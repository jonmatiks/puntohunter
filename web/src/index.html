Miner settings

Username
<address>.<worker>
Password
none, or a fleet password of 8 characters or more to make your fleet private

Give each miner a worker name. Your username is your Bitcoin address, a dot, and a worker name: address.worker

Worker names take up to 64 letters, digits, dots, dashes, and underscores.

Without a fleet password, anyone who knows your address can open your fleet page. Set the same one on each device to make it private: in the password field over Stratum V1, or after a # in the username over Stratum V2, which has no password field: address.worker#password. A fleet password takes no +.

Stratum V2 authority key

Pin this key in your miner to trust the pool's V2 certificate. The V2 URLs above carry it already.

On (UTC) the pool switches to a new key:

A change of key is announced here, with the new key and the day it takes over, ahead of the switch. Keep the current key until then: a V2 device pinned to the new key cannot connect before the switch, and one pinned to the old key cannot connect after it, until you enter the new key or the new V2 URL. Stratum V1 devices are not affected.
